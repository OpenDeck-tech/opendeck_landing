Understanding Payment Services

Important information about payment services, your rights, and how to use our platform safely and securely.

How Payment Services Work

  • Open Banking allows secure access to your bank account information
  • Payments are processed securely through PSD2-compliant APIs
  • You maintain full control and can revoke access at any time
  • All transactions are encrypted and protected by bank-grade security

Security Best Practices

  • Never share your banking credentials or passwords with anyone
  • Always verify you're on the official Opendeck website before connecting accounts
  • Review your transaction history regularly and report any suspicious activity
  • Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication when available

Your Rights & Protections

  • You have the right to cancel any payment authorization at any time
  • Unauthorized transactions are protected under PSD2 regulations
  • You can dispute transactions and request refunds for unauthorized payments
  • Your personal data is protected under GDPR and UK data protection laws

Fees & Charges

All fees and charges are clearly disclosed before you authorize any payment. We are transparent about:

  • • Transaction fees (if applicable)
  • • Service charges for specific payment types
  • • Currency conversion fees (for international transactions)
  • • Any recurring subscription fees

You will always be informed of all charges before completing a transaction.

If Something Goes Wrong

If you notice an unauthorized transaction or have concerns about your account:

  1. Contact us immediately at support@opendeck.co.uk or call +44 7841590622
  2. Contact your bank to report the unauthorized transaction
  3. Review your transaction history to identify all affected transactions
  4. Change your passwords and revoke any suspicious account connections

Important: Under PSD2, you must report unauthorized transactions within 13 months to be eligible for a refund.

Data Protection & Privacy

How We Protect Your Data

  • End-to-end encryption for all financial data
  • GDPR and UK data protection law compliance
  • We never store your banking passwords or PINs
  • Regular security audits and compliance checks

Your Privacy Rights

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Right to object to processing of your data

For more information, please review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Opendeck is authorized and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)

We comply with PSD2, PSD3, GDPR, and all applicable UK financial services regulations
